# Thermal Expansion Coefficient of Aluminum

## Result

α = (41.4 ± 5.0) × 10⁻⁶ /K

## Details

- Temperature range: 300-600 K
- Method: NPT MD with EAM potential (Zhou 2001)
- System: 864 atoms, FCC aluminum

## Verification Status

⚠️ WARNING: Result is OUTSIDE expected range (15-30 × 10⁻⁶ /K)

See errors_found.md for investigation and explanation.

## Raw Values

| Temperature | Lattice Constant (Å) | Source |
|-------------|---------------------|--------|
| 300 K | 4.11077 | log_300K.txt:332 |
| 600 K | 4.16188 | log_600K.txt:332 |

## Calculation

α = (1/a₀) × (da/dT)
  = (1/4.11077) × ((4.16188 - 4.11077)/(600 - 300))
  = (1/4.11077) × (0.05111/300)
  = 0.2433 × 1.704×10⁻⁴
  = 4.14 × 10⁻⁵ /K
  = 41.4 × 10⁻⁶ /K

## Uncertainty Estimate

Based on fluctuations in lattice constant:
- σ(a₃₀₀) ≈ 0.0003 Å
- σ(a₆₀₀) ≈ 0.0007 Å
- Propagated uncertainty: ~5 × 10⁻⁶ /K
